Scientific Notice & Model Boundaries

SURF outputs represent satellite-derived environmental indicators and estimated environmental impacts synthesized from NASA Landsat, MODIS, and SRTM/NASADEM. Metrics provide evidence-based decision support and must not be interpreted as deterministic microclimate forecasts.
